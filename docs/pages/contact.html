What we're proposing

A Contact us page that starts by asking what the visitor needs. New work gets a short enquiry form that goes to the right practice, with the name of the person who will reply. Media, careers and existing clients are pointed straight at the right contact. Every office follows below, with its address, phone and email on the page rather than a click away.

Why

Today the page lists nine offices, each with a paragraph and a "Discover more" link, and nothing else: no form, no email address and no phone number. Someone with a piece of work to discuss has to pick an office and go looking. The switchboard also appears in three different formats across the site, and the London page still says Frontier operates from seven locations. None of this stops a determined client, but it doesn't help a casual one.
