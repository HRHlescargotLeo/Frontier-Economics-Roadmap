What we're proposing

A hub for one live question. It opens with Frontier's view in a few lines and the numbers that frame the debate, then lists everything Frontier has published on it in date order (reports, articles, news, events), filterable by type. The people behind the work sit alongside, with a Follow button so readers hear about the next piece.

Why

The North Sea work is some of Frontier's most visible this year: two independent reports, an article by the Chair and a news release. On the live site they are four separate pages in three different lists, and nothing joins them up. Someone arriving from a newspaper link to one of them has no obvious way to find the other three.

You chose . This prototype builds out the North Sea hub only; every hub would use the same template.

North Sea oil and gas

Our view in brief

  • More UK production would do little to lower prices, which are set in international markets.
  • It could reduce import dependence somewhat, mainly for gas, but not quickly enough to help with near-term shocks.
  • The case for more production rests on growth, jobs, tax revenues, emissions and the UK's climate leadership, and on how those are traded off.
