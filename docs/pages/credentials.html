What we're proposing

A library of short, dated summaries of Frontier's engagements, each one a few lines: who it was for (where that can be said), the question, what Frontier did and how it turned out. It can be searched and filtered by sector, expertise, country and date. A second view lists the experts who have given evidence and where, for solicitors looking to instruct one.

Why

There are around 90 case studies on the site, undated and without search. A lot of Frontier's most persuasive work doesn't appear in them at all: Trucks, BritNed and Power Cables surface only in Jon Adlard's biography. Case studies take time to write and approve, which presumably explains why there are so few; a two-line summary is a much smaller ask of a partner, and a hundred of them say more than a dozen long pieces.
