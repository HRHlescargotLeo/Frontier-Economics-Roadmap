Prototype 5 of 5 · Events · idea 32

What we're proposing

An events page that leads with what's coming up, lets people register on the page and add the event to their calendar, and keeps past events underneath with slides or recordings where there are some. When nothing is scheduled in a location, it offers invitations instead of an empty list.

Why

On 1 October the live list opened with Berlin Dispute Resolution Days, held two days earlier, and nothing upcoming. Past and future events share one list behind a Past/Present switch. Events are one of the few places a prospective client meets Frontier in person, so the page is worth more than its current state suggests.
