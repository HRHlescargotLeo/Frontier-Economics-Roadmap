Five ideas for frontier-economics.com

Clickable prototypes of five improvements to the Frontier Economics website. They are about what happens after someone reads a piece of Frontier's work: finding more of it, checking the track record behind it, getting in touch with the right person and hearing about the next piece. They sit alongside the Facelift project and leave its components alone.

The prototypes

  1. Talk to us
    1

    Talk to the right person

    A Contact us page that asks what you need and routes you to the right person, with an enquiry form and every office's details in one place. Sector and expertise pages name the people to talk to.

    Try: open the Competition page, choose Contact Jon, and send the form with a field left empty first.

    Open prototype
  2. Insights library
    2

    Find any piece of Frontier thinking

    One library for articles, news, reports, case studies, consultation responses and newsletters, with search and filters that can be shared as a link. Plus a site search that dates and groups its results, and an article page that says when it was written.

    Try: filter to Energy reports from 2026, copy the link, then search the site for "hydrogen".

    Open prototype
  3. Track record
    3

    Proof, not prose

    A searchable library of short, dated engagement summaries, a view of Frontier's testifying experts for law firms, and a case study template with an at-a-glance panel, the outcome up front and a route to the case lead.

    Try: filter to Competition, open the Vodafone / Three engagement, then choose Discuss a similar issue.

    Open prototype
  4. Big questions
    4

    A home for each big question

    Hot Topics brought into the menu and rebuilt as dated hubs, starting with North Sea oil and gas. A report landing page for the two briefing notes, and a models and tools section built on the COMET page.

    Try: open the North Sea hub, follow it, filter the timeline to reports, then open part two.

    Open prototype
  5. Stay in touch
    5

    Stay close to Frontier

    A Frontier Focus sign-up that lets people choose topics, a list of what you follow, a dated archive, and an events page that leads with what's coming up and takes registrations.

    Try: sign up for Energy and Competition, confirm, then register for the November briefing and add it to your calendar.

    Open prototype

How to review

  • This is the designed version: the same pages and behaviour as the greyscale prototypes, with Frontier's colours, type and photography applied. Condensed headings use a free stand-in for Frontier's licensed display face.
  • Everything interactive works: the enquiry routes and form, filters, search, the credentials library, the topic timeline, sign-up and event registration. Links to pages outside these five go nowhere by design.
  • Use the bar at the top to move between prototypes. Switch Notes on (top right) to see what each prototype proposes and why, plus notes on behaviour (yellow) and open questions for Frontier (red). Notes start switched off.
  • What you follow carries between prototypes for the session, so the pieces can be tried together.
  • Titles, people, roles and recent dates come from frontier-economics.com. Older dates, credentials, upcoming events, office details outside London and anything in [brackets] are samples.
  • The Facelift project is redesigning the homepage banner, featured items, accordion, image and text tiles, people profiles and the people directory. These prototypes link to them rather than redesign them.
