What we're proposing

Hot Topics in the main menu, as a list of dated topic hubs that can be filtered by area. Each card says how many pieces sit behind it and when it was last updated, so a reader can tell a live debate from an archive. North Sea oil and gas becomes the first new hub.

Why

Frontier already has a Hot Topics section with seven collections, including the Cunliffe review, COMET and the Big Questions podcast. It isn't in the main menu or the footer, the collections carry no dates, and the most current issue, North Sea production, doesn't have one. The Cunliffe page still describes the review as published "earlier this summer"; that was July 2025.
