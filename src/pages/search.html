Prototype 2 of 5 · Site search · idea 17

What we're proposing

Search results that lead with the pages people are most likely to want (the Energy sector page, the HyLO model, a topic hub), then group everything else by type with a count on each tab. Every result shows its date and type, results can be sorted by relevance or date, and people appear as their own tab.

Why

A search for "hydrogen" on the live site returns 147 results, eight to a page across 19 pages, with no dates, no indication of type and no way to sort or filter. Page one includes a piece framed around the 2020 EU Hydrogen Strategy and work for BEIS, a department replaced in 2023; the September 2026 article on blue and turquoise hydrogen isn't on it. Someone checking what Frontier thinks now would reasonably conclude it stopped thinking about hydrogen some years ago.
