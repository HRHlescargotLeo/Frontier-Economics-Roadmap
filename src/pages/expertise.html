What we're proposing

Every sector and expertise page names two or three people to talk to, with a route straight into the enquiry form and a link to everyone working in that area. Expertise pages also carry a short recognition strip: rankings, and the courts and tribunals where Frontier's experts have given evidence. The rest of the page stays as it is, including the capability list the Facelift is redesigning.

Why

The live Energy and Competition pages name nobody. Competition ends with four related articles and no prompt to get in touch. Frontier's Lexology 2026 listings exist only as a news item, and Jon Adlard's Future Leader listing only on his profile. A general counsel comparing firms is presumably trying to work out who they would be dealing with, and at the moment the page doesn't say.
