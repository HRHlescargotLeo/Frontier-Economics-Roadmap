What we're proposing

A Models and tools section with a page for each of Frontier's models, built on the COMET page that already exists: the question the model answers, what it covers, how clients use it, the engagements it has been used on and who to talk to. The other models get the same page, once the modelling teams have written them up.

Why

COMET has a good page in Hot Topics, with features, applications, a brochure and a named contact. SPIRIT, HyLO, the gas flow model and the customer behaviour model appear only as items inside the Energy page. Proprietary models are one of the more tangible things an economic consultancy can point to, and at the moment most of Frontier's are hidden in a list.
