What we're proposing

A way to subscribe to Frontier Focus, choosing the sectors and expertise areas you care about and how often you want to hear. Anything you follow elsewhere on the site (a topic hub, a sector, an expert) shows up here too. The archive of past editions sits underneath, with dates. A compact version of the sign-up appears at the end of articles and topic hubs.

Why

Frontier Focus is described as "our newsletter" and has eight pages of past editions, but there is no way to sign up for it: not on that page, not on the articles we checked and not in the footer. The editions aren't dated either. Every reader who finishes an article and wants more currently has to remember to come back.
