Vodafone and Three UK: clearing a complex merger through efficiency-based arguments

Published [June 2025, sample date] · 4 min read

Case study image 16:9

The question

The merger of Vodafone UK and Three UK, first announced in 2023, would take the UK from four mobile network operators to three. That raised familiar concerns for competition authorities: fewer retail competitors, potentially higher prices, and effects on network access for other retailers. Could the case be made on investment and quality instead?

What we did

Frontier advised Vodafone throughout the CMA's review. [Two or three sentences on the analysis, drawn from the live page's "Shifting the frame" and "Ensuring credible commitments" sections.]

  • Shifted the frame from short-term prices to long-term quality and investment
  • Helped make commitments credible and benefits measurable

The outcome

The CMA cleared the merger in December 2024, signalling a new willingness to consider efficiency gains in investment-heavy sectors. The deal completed in 2025.
